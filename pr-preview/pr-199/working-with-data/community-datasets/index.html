    Community datasets

    This page shows six real metadata records, built for datasets that are published on repository.nfdi4cat.org, NFDI4Cat's data repository ("Repo4Cat"). Nothing here is a schema reference. Each record shows what a finished CoreMeta4Cat description looks like once real data goes into it, so you have something concrete to compare your own dataset against.

    All six records are part of CoreMeta4Cat's test suite (tests/data/valid/), so they are validated against the schema on every change. The usage patterns are small excerpts of these records.

    Where these records come from

    The CoreMeta4Cat records are not part of the Repo4Cat deposits. A deposit keeps the files as its authors submitted them, so each dataset link on this page leads to the source data, not to a CoreMeta4Cat record. Some deposits also contain metadata files of their own: Dataset 1, for example, includes JSON files for its experiments. These are not CoreMeta4Cat records.

    The records on this page were created by the CoreMeta4Cat team with the help of a large language model, from the files of each deposit and from the publication it accompanies. Each section below offers its record for download as YAML, JSON and RDF. If you find an error in a record, please open an issue.

    # Dataset Topics Activities in the record Sources

    2 P-modified Pd catalysts for selective hydrogenation heterogeneous catalysis 2 syntheses, 5 characterizations, 4 reactions deposit + article
    3 Palladium phosphide catalysts for carbonylation heterogeneous catalysis 2 syntheses, 5 characterizations, 3 reactions deposit + article

    "Deposit + article" means the repository files alone did not contain everything CoreMeta4Cat asks for. The missing pieces (synthesis routes, instruments, software, reactor) came from the publication.

    How each record is identified

    CoreMeta4Cat identifies data with vocabulary terms on two levels (see Model Structure). At the conceptual level (Layer 1), a dataset is classified by its catalysis research field and by the type of each reaction it describes. At the class level (Layer 2), each activity is typed by a term for its technique or method, and each reactor by its reactor type. The table lists the terms each record uses; the usage patterns show how they are written.

    # Conceptual level: research field · reaction type Class level: techniques and methods
    1 heterogeneous catalysis (voc4cat:0007001) · hydrogenation (voc4cat:0000260), CO2 hydrogenation (voc4cat:0000259) gas chromatography (voc4cat:0000130)
    2 heterogeneous catalysis (voc4cat:0007001) · hydrogenation (voc4cat:0000260) impregnation (voc4cat:0007028), BET analysis (enm:0000064), DRIFTS (CHMO:0000645), ICP-AES (CHMO:0000267), infrared spectroscopy (CHMO:0000630), powder XRD (CHMO:0000158)
    3 heterogeneous catalysis (voc4cat:0007001) · carbonylation (voc4cat:0000247) impregnation (voc4cat:0007028), DRIFTS (CHMO:0000645), powder XRD (CHMO:0000158), transmission electron microscopy (voc4cat:0000078), XPS (CHMO:0000404); CO chemisorption has no term yet
    4 heterogeneous catalysis (voc4cat:0007001) · CO2 hydrogenation (voc4cat:0000259) co-precipitation (voc4cat:0007795), impregnation (voc4cat:0007028), X-ray fluorescence (voc4cat:0000067), density functional theory (voc4cat:0000215), fixed-bed reactor (coremeta4cat:FixedBedReactor); the machine-learned-potential screenings have no term yet

    Chemicals, such as reactants, products and the elements of a catalyst, are identified by ChEBI terms.

    Dataset 1 — CO/CO₂ methanation over nickel

    Dataset for Publication: Reaction Kinetics of CO and CO2 Methanation over Nickel Schmider, D.; Maier, L.; Deutschmann, O. (2025) — Institute for Catalysis Research and Technology (IKFT), KIT Related publication: Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. (2021), doi:10.1021/acs.iecr.1c00389

    This dataset reports steady-state CO and CO₂ methanation kinetics measured over 16 nickel catalysts (supports Al₂O₃, SiO₂, ZrO₂, TiO₂ and MgAl₂O₄; nickel loading 5–41 wt%) in fixed-bed reactors. The 16 runs split into three reaction types depending on the feed: CO methanation, CO₂ methanation, and a mixed CO/CO₂ feed.

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 1 Characterization (was_generated_by_characterization): the outlet gas analysis used for every run.
    • 3 CatalyticReactions (is_about_activity): one per reaction type, each listing all catalysts tested under that feed and the temperature range explored.

    Grouping by reaction type and technique keeps the record proportional to how the study is organised: 4 pieces instead of 16.

    Patterns from this dataset: Classifying with vocabulary terms

    Dataset 2 — Phosphorus-modified Pd catalysts for selective hydrogenation

    Support Engineering with Phosphorus: Tuning the Palladium-Catalyzed Selective Hydrogenation of α,β-Unsaturated Aldehydes Rang, F.; Hanf, S.; Holtermann, B.; Eggeler, Y.; Barth, S.; Grunwaldt, J.-D. (2026) — KIT

    This dataset investigates how phosphorus modification of the support tunes Pd-catalyzed selective hydrogenation, across 10 Pd/P catalysts (Al₂O₃ and SiO₂ supports, 0–5 wt% P). It combines five characterization techniques with catalytic testing of two substrates (cinnamaldehyde and citral) on both supports.

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 2 Synthesis (was_generated_by_synthesis): the phosphorus modification of alumina and silica with NH₄H₂PO₄, then the Pd impregnation of the modified and unmodified supports. The modified supports link the two steps: they are the output of the first and an input of the second.
    • 5 Characterizations (was_generated_by_characterization): one per technique (BET, ICP-AES, PXRD, DRIFT, IR), each covering every catalyst that technique was applied to.
    • 4 CatalyticReactions (is_about_activity): one per substrate and support.

    Sources: the deposit has the characterization results and the catalytic test data; the two synthesis steps, the support pretreatment and the precursor amounts come from the article (Rang et al., ChemRxiv 2026). Not yet in the record: the sample preparation for each characterization technique.

    Patterns from this dataset: One technique, many samples

    Dataset 3 — Palladium phosphide catalysts for carbonylation

    Carbonylation catalysis of aryl halides through active-site engineering Neyyathala, A.; Jung, F.; Feldmann, C.; Barth, S.; Grunwaldt, J.-D.; Jevtovik, I.; Schunk, S. A.; Dolcet, P.; Gross, S.; Hanf, S. (2025) — KIT and collaborators

    This dataset compares a phosphorus-modified palladium phosphide catalyst (Pd₃P/SiO₂) with an unmodified Pd/SiO₂ reference across 31 batch-reactor carbonylation runs, mostly alkoxycarbonylation of aryl iodides plus a few phenoxy- and aminocarbonylation runs. It is characterized by five techniques; PXRD also covers two further loading variants, and STEM a spent catalyst.

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 2 Synthesis (was_generated_by_synthesis): Pd₃P/SiO₂ and the Pd/SiO₂ reference, both by incipient wetness impregnation and thermal treatment in hydrogen; they share one preparation method.
    • 5 Characterizations (was_generated_by_characterization): PXRD, DRIFT (with its argon pretreatment), CO chemisorption, XPS and STEM (both with their sample preparation).
    • 3 CatalyticReactions (is_about_activity): one per reaction type.

    Sources: the deposit has the characterization and catalytic test data; the synthesis, the DRIFT pretreatment and the sample preparation for STEM and XPS come from the article (Neyyathala et al., J. Catal. 456 (2026) 116733). The article gives synthesis amounts only for the 5 wt% Pd catalysts, so the 1 and 10 wt% Pd₃P/SiO₂ samples measured by PXRD have no synthesis in the record. The catalysts were used in the catalytic tests as synthesized, without further activation.

    Dataset 4 — Cu–Ga–Al mixed oxides for CO₂ to methanol and DME

    Copper Gallium Aluminum Mixed Metal Oxides as Alternative Catalyst Candidates for Efficient Conversion of Carbon Dioxide to Methanol and Dimethyl Ether Weber, S. (2025) — hte GmbH Related publication: Weber et al., ACS Catal. 2025, 15, 18857, doi:10.1021/acscatal.5c03876

    This study combines computational screening with synthesis and high-throughput testing. DFT and two machine-learned interatomic potentials (an Open Catalyst Project model and MACE-MPA-0) give OH and CO binding energies for doped oxide sites and 37 intermetallic compositions. 76 Cu–Ga–Al hydrotalcite precursors are co-precipitated and analysed by XRF; 13 of them are screened for CO₂/CO hydrogenation, and seven optimized catalysts are tested under 17 conditions at 80 bar in a 16-fold fixed-bed reactor.

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 4 Synthesis (was_generated_by_synthesis): the co-precipitation route (with the article's worked example), the first-generation catalyst, and the second-generation catalyst in two chained steps (co-precipitation, then Ga impregnation).
    • 1 Characterization: XRF of the precursor library.
    • 3 Simulations: DFT binding energies and the two machine-learned-potential screenings.
    • 2 CatalyticReactions (is_about_activity): the 13-sample screening and the tests of the optimized catalysts.

    Sources: the deposit has the computed binding energies, compositions and the test protocol; synthesis route, instruments, reactor and DFT code come from the article. Not stated in either: precursor masses except for one worked example, and the product analysis method (presumably in the article's supporting information). Both fields are recommended, so the record is valid without them.

    Patterns from this dataset: A synthesis with precursors, amounts and a typed preparation method · A preparation in two steps · A simulation with a typed method and calculated properties

    Dataset 5 — NH₃ decomposition over Ru/Ni-BCZY, experiment and microkinetics

    Dataset for Publication: Experimental and Microkinetic Investigation of Thermo-Catalytic Ammonia Decomposition over a Ba-promoted Ru/Ni-BCZY Catalyst for Use in Ammonia-fed Protonic Ceramic Cells Davari, S.; Chacko, R.; Bastek, T.; Lott, P.; Dailly, J.; Angeli, S.; Deutschmann, O. (2025) — KIT and EIFER Related publication: Davari et al., Appl. Catal. A 708 (2025) 120571, doi:10.1016/j.apcata.2025.120571

    Six Ru/Ba catalysts on Ni-BCZY, Ni-YSZ and YSZ supports are tested for thermocatalytic NH₃ decomposition as light-off curves in a packed bed (500–3000 ppm NH₃ in Ar, 1 bar, GHSV 50,000 h⁻¹). A microkinetic packed-bed model (DETCHEM) is fitted to the experiments and then used for reactor profiles, turnover frequencies and a parameter study.

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 2 Synthesis (was_generated_by_synthesis): the cermet supports (tape casting and sintering), then Ru and Ba by incipient wetness impregnation.
    • 1 Characterization: H₂-TPR of the Ni-BCZY cermet.
    • 2 Simulations: the microkinetic model compared with the experiments, and the parameter study.
    • 1 CatalyticReaction (is_about_activity): the light-off experiments of all six catalysts.

    Sources: the deposit has the light-off curves and simulation results, organised by figure; catalysts, reactor, analyser, software and the TPR conditions come from the article. Not stated: precursor masses (the article gives target loadings).

    Patterns from this dataset: A dataset, its classification and its activities · A measurement with a typed technique · Catalysts, reactants and products · Reactor, conditions and product analysis · Values, ranges and fractions with units

    Dataset 6 — Amine transaminase molecular dynamics

    Datasets for Publication: Navigating the Sequence-Function Landscape: AI-Driven Discovery of Unseen and Synergistic Mutations in an Amine Transaminase - Molecular Dynamic Simulations Doerr, M.; Weigmann, K.; Schulig, L. (2026) — University of Greifswald Related publication: Weigmann et al., ACS Catal. (2025), doi:10.1021/acscatal.5c04248

    The computational part of a protein-engineering study: an amine transaminase variant (3FCR-4M) and two mutants are prepared, the PLP-bound substrate (S)-1-(2-chlorophenyl)ethanamine is docked, the complexes are simulated by molecular dynamics, and MM/GBSA gives relative binding free energies. The deposit consists of the software archives of each step (about 33 GB).

    The CoreMeta4Cat record groups this into:

    • 4 Simulations (was_generated_by_simulation): protein preparation, induced-fit docking, molecular dynamics and MM/GBSA. There is no CatalyticReaction; the enzyme kinetics are not part of this deposit.

    Sources: the deposit's file descriptions name the software and the variants; the article gives the enzyme and substrate identities and the MM/GBSA results. Not stated in the main text: MD settings (in the article's supporting information and the archives).

    Patterns from this dataset: Comparing variants with relative values · A simulation step that produces structures, not properties

    A note on grouping

    None of these datasets describes one catalyst measured once. Real studies screen many catalysts, or run the same technique across a whole series. Writing one Characterization per catalyst, or one CatalyticReaction per run, would make a record balloon to dozens of near-identical entries.

    The records therefore group by what was actually shared: one Characterization per technique, covering every sample it was applied to, and one CatalyticReaction per reaction type or condition set, covering every catalyst tested under it. Values such as temperature are given as the range explored, not one value per run. A record then grows with the number of distinct methods and reaction types in a study, not with the number of data points.
